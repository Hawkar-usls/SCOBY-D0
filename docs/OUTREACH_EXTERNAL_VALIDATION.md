# SCOBY-D0 — External validation outreach

## Primary target: CEDRE (France)

Why this is the first external target: CEDRE evaluates spill-response sorbents and equipment, including standardized, custom and deployment testing under environmental variables such as salinity, current and temperature.

Contact: `contact@cedre.fr`

### Suggested subject

`Open R&D proposal: low-cost retrievable bacterial-cellulose sorbent boom for marine oil spills`

### Draft email

Dear CEDRE team,

I am developing an open, evidence-gated research project called **SCOBY-D0**, focused on a low-cost retrievable bacterial-cellulose sorbent architecture for oil-on-water cleanup.

The concept is not being presented as a field-ready product. We have pre-registered a staged validation path from 50×50 mm coupons to 100×100 mm marine pads and, only if the material passes salinity, dynamic-water, retention and retrieval gates, to a 1 m modular boom cartridge.

Two processing branches are being advanced as finalists:

1. a freeze-dried bacterial-cellulose pad with minimal environmentally compatible hydrophobic surface engineering, as the performance lead;
2. a low-compaction air-dried bacterial-cellulose pad, as the cost-down challenger.

The intended field architecture is a retrievable outer mesh/sleeve containing modular BC inserts, so the deployed sorbent is recovered as one object rather than released as loose material. After retrieval, the cartridge would be drained/pressed in containment and the recovered oil/water fraction separated.

Our current primary metrics are oil/water selectivity, oil retention during retrieval, material retrieval fraction, compression recovery, salinity/dynamic penalties, and ultimately cost per kilogram of recovered oil.

I would be grateful for your advice on whether this staged protocol is suitable for later independent evaluation at CEDRE, and what minimum sample size, material characterization and preliminary data you would expect before considering standardized, custom or deployment testing.

The project repository is public:
https://github.com/Hawkar-usls/SCOBY-D0

Key preregistered artifacts include the marine dynamic saltwater gate and the M1A finalist selection in the repository root.

I would be happy to provide a concise technical dossier, sample specifications and the full measurement schema.

Best regards,
Hawkar
SCOBY-D0 / JANUS research ecosystem
