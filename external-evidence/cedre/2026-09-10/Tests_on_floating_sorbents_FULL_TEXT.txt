                                Tests on floating sorbents
In the framework of its public service mission, Cedre assesses the efficiency of response products which
are liable to be used in the environment in case of accidental pollution due to petroleum products.

Methodology
    The testing procedure is based on the following tests:

    •    Characteristics of the sorbents
         The characterization will depend on the product nature (surface density, granulometry…)

    •    Measurement of the water sorption capability of floating sorbent
         The sorbent capacity on water is assessed using the same methodology as the one applied for
         hydrocarbons. These measurements are performed by weighing the sorbent saturated with
         water.

    •    Measurement of the hydrocarbons sorption capability of floating sorbent according the
         protocol 1 of the NFT 90-360 norm
         This method aims at measuring the sorption capacity at saturation towards hydrocarbons of
         low and medium viscosity. This method relates exclusively to hydrophobic floating sorbent.
         This protocol applies to bulk sorbents (type A), sorbent in the form of sheets, rolls and mat
         (type B and C), as well as the wrapped sorbent (type D and E), for which the test is carried out
         on the material extracted from its envelope.
         The sorption capacities are assessed assuming a low level of agitation time during 20 minutes,
         followed by a draining period of 30 minutes.

    •    Measurement of the maximum hydrocarbons sorption capability of floating sorbent
         according the protocol 2 of the NFT 90-360 norm (Optional test)
         This method aims at measuring the maximum hydrocarbon sorption capacity over short and,
         optionally, long immersion period, determined for contact times of 15 minutes and 24 hours
         respectively and a draining period of 30 seconds.

         This method replicates the test conditions of the ASTM standard designated F726-99 in
         terms of immersion and draining period and allows comparison with the results obtained
         with this norm.

These tests can be conducted using three different petroleum products: Arabian light crude oil topped
at 110°C (BAL 110) and/or Diesel oil and/or 5W30 oil. The characteristics of these three oils are as
follows:

        Type of hydrocarbons                Density at 20°C               Viscosity at 20°C ((mPa.s)
              BAL 110                         0.88 ± 0.02                           42-45
              Diesel oil                      0.83 ± 0.02                             10
              5W30 oil                        0.85 ± 0.02                         100 - 160
