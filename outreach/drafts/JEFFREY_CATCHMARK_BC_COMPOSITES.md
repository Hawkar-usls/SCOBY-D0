Your work on bacterial-cellulose production, cellulose/nanocellulose characterization and polysaccharide composites makes your perspective especially relevant to the architecture of the project.

The central causal rule in our composite branch is: process → composition → microstructure → mesostructure → bulk property → function. A material is not promoted because it looks different or because a simulation predicts improvement. Chitin, drying, electrosynthesis and other modifications are kept as independent branches with matched controls and separate claim ceilings.

If you are willing to take a brief look, I would value criticism of this experimental hierarchy—especially whether the baseline and structure/property measurements are sufficient to prevent false reinforcement claims, and which BC characterization step you would add before comparing modified branches.

The repository is early R&D and is intentionally designed to preserve negative results and uncertainty.
